About

I've spent more than twenty years fixing, building and measuring things. I started in mechanical contracting and electronics repair in Alaska, worked on cinema systems and radio science, then moved into energy data and automation. Today I work in nuclear quality, where careful records and calibrated measurements matter as much as the hardware.

I like finding solutions, making things and helping people. I'm happiest bridging the gap between the theoretical and the practical.

Provenance

Python · signal processing · forensics

Recording forensics: where and when. It locates a recording from the Doppler slide of a passing aircraft matched to public ADS-B tracks, and dates it from the mains hum (ENF) in the audio or in the flicker of lights on video. A reference rebuilt from public seismic stations covers the Western US grid. Built as a privacy check that runs entirely on your own computer.
