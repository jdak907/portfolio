About

I've spent more than twenty years in hands-on technical work. I started in Alaska, in signs, mechanical supply and construction project management. From there I moved into radio and electronics repair, cinema engineering, and SDR experiments at HAARP. Then came energy pricing and data automation, and today I do metrology and quality work in nuclear.

The common thread is that I like finding solutions, making things and helping people. I'm at home in startups, where I build the lab, write the procedure and train the team. I care about bridging the gap between theory and practice.
